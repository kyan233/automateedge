Questions

Where do the businesses come from?

We build a list for your area from public sources such as Companies House and business listings, check each firm's size, then contact them by email or phone with the offer of a free IT and security check with you.

What if someone doesn't turn up?

You don't pay. You only pay for meetings that actually happen with a business that fits your brief.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. We agree your brief and area by email, and you can stop at any time. We invoice after each held meeting.

Which areas do you cover?

Anywhere in the UK. We book businesses near you, in the towns you tell us you can serve.

What do you say to the businesses?

We offer a free, no-obligation 20-minute IT and security check with a local IT company. We never promise anything on your behalf that you haven't agreed to.
