Questions

What if someone doesn't turn up?

You don't pay. You only pay for meetings that happen with a business that fits your brief.

Who decides a meeting counts?

It counts when the business fits your brief, you meet the owner, a director, a partner or an office or operations manager, and the meeting lasts at least 10 minutes in person, by phone or by video. If you think one shouldn't count, tell us within 3 working days.

What do you say to them?

We offer a free, no-obligation 20-minute IT and security check with a local IT company. That's you.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. You agree a one-page agreement by replying to an email, and either of us can stop with 7 days' notice. We invoice weekly, only for meetings that happened.

How many meetings can I get?

It depends on your area and brief. We agree a monthly cap with you, so you're never booked beyond what you can take on.

Is cold outreach legal in the UK?

Yes, when it's done properly. We only email limited companies and LLPs at business addresses, every email has an easy opt-out, we check phone numbers against the Corporate Telephone Preference Service, and we've paid our ICO data protection fee. See our privacy notice.

Is AutomateEdge the same as AutomationEdge?

No. AutomateEdge (automateedge.ai) is a UK appointment-setting service for IT support firms, based in Woking. AutomationEdge is an unrelated automation software company.
